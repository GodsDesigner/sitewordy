How it works

Five minutes a day is plenty.

Pick a list

Choose your child's grade level, a phonics skill, or your own words.

Play a round

10 or 20 words at a time. Words they haven't learned yet come first.

Watch them grow

A word counts as learned after 3 correct answers. Collect stars along the way.
